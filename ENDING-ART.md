The production files are 1536 × 1024 WebP, encoded at quality 86 from the generated PNGs. The compositions retain the game's muted olive, tobacco brown, and amber photographic palette. They are intended for ending scenes, route scenes, and social previews.

The corresponding social previews are 1200 × 800 JPEGs encoded at quality 85: `docs/share/11/preview.jpg` (relief), `12/preview.jpg` (witness), `13/preview.jpg` (rescue), `14/preview.jpg` (vacancy), and `15/preview.jpg` (transmission). Lighting and palette: deep muted olive-black shadows, tobacco brown wood, dusty sepia and small warm amber light; restrained desaturated colors. Quiet uncanny exchange, no gore.
