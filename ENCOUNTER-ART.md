# Walking encounter artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. The existing landscape and palette are preserved. Each of the twelve walking encounters now has a distinct illustration in the same scratched-film, muddy olive and tarnished-gold direction as the existing field plates. The original radio, tracks and lantern plates belong exclusively to `first-call`, `paired-tracks` and `lamp-keeper`, respectively. Original PNGs remain outside the repository; the shipped files are WebP encodings with no composition edits.

## Individual encounter additions

Nine additional illustrations were generated with the built-in tool, one call per scene, plus a follow-up edit to ground the future-call radio in the furrow described by the encounter. The initial prompt for each is the shared direction below followed by `Subject: ` and its scene description; the final future-call edit is recorded separately. Each is shipped as a 1536 × 1024 WebP. Existing encounter illustrations and all background artwork remain unchanged. Desktop scene framing and individual focal positions keep the important details visible; the hanging lantern and supply tin retain a vertical composition.

Shared direction:

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: production encounter artwork for DEBRIEF, a rural ecological horror game. Landscape 3:2. Tactile analog photographic illustration with realistic worn materials, fine grain, subtle scratches and faded photo edges. Palette strictly subdued forest green #141d18, muddy olive, damp brown earth, dirty parchment highlights and small tarnished-gold lights. Deep green night, weak warm flashlight illumination, restrained dread, grounded cinematic realism. Compose the defining subject across the middle horizontal band so it remains readable cropped to a wide mobile banner. No lettering, text, logos, watermark, UI, neon, glossy fantasy, gore, or graphic injury.

Directly beneath it at mid-frame is an open battered rectangular supply tin, its lid tipped back, a small indistinct brass glint inside. Warm flame lights the empty tin rim and wet leaves. No person or silhouette anywhere: this is the first quiet discovery of the lamp and supplies. Oblique close view, lantern and open tin clearly visible together, still leaves, dark unoccupied corridor beyond.
