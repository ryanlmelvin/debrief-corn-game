# Walking encounter artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. The existing landscape and palette are preserved. These three illustrations use the same scratched-film, muddy olive and tarnished-gold direction as the existing field plates. Original PNGs remain outside the repository; the shipped files are WebP encodings with no composition edits.

## docs/assets/encounter-radio.webp

Use case: illustration-story. Production artwork for DEBRIEF, a rural ecological horror game. Landscape 3:2, no text, logos, watermark, lettering, UI or border. Tactile analog photographic illustration: scratched aging film negative, fine grain, worn faded corners, subdued forest green #141d18, muddy olive, dirty parchment highlights, tarnished gold, damp soil and physically believable materials. A weak warm flashlight beam against deep green dusk. Restrained dread, grounded cinematic realism, no glossy fantasy, no neon, no graphic injury. Match the game's existing gritty photographs of an abandoned pump shed and root-filled drainage ditch. Keep the main subject within the middle horizontal band so it can crop to a wide mobile banner. Subject: an old battered olive hand-held field radio lying in wet furrows between towering corn rows, its black coiled microphone lead trailing into the dark stalks. Rusty controls, water beading on the grille, one dim amber indicator. No legible markings. Low close camera; a narrow empty row recedes behind the radio. A mundane object made unsettling.
