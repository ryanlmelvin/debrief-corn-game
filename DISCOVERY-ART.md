# Discovery illustrations

Nine individual discovery illustrations were generated with the built-in image generation tool, one call per asset. Each finished image was visually inspected against the canonical exhibit and its two alternate discovery scenes. The existing corner, pump-house, drainage-channel and specimen-study illustrations were inspected as style and subject references; no existing artwork or background palette was changed.

All final assets are 1536 × 1024 pixels. Each ships as a WebP encoding at quality 86, with no resizing, cropping, recoloring or other composition edits. Original generated PNGs remain outside the repository. These assets contain original game-adaptation imagery and no manuscript text. The established scene art remains available for original discovery presentations.

The final project paths and exact generation prompts follow.
